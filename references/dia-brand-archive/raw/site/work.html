Dia is built for work

We’re getting Dia ready for companies of all sizes.

Dia is a modern, AI-native browser that helps teams work faster, built with strong security controls and enterprise-grade management in mind.

Dia brings secure, native AI workflows into your browsing and apps, so you get productivity and compliance in one place. With Dia, there is no context switching, fewer copy-and-paste loops, and strong admin controls for data, access, and updates.
