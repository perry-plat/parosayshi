# Night bird audio sources

The night-mode contact birds use three genuine owl field recordings from
Freesound. Each source is released under Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0).

- `owl-hoot-breviceps-cc0.mp3` — “Owl Hoot” by Breviceps:
  https://freesound.org/s/465697/
- `barred-owl-distant-cc0.mp3` — “Birds of Prey - Barred Owl Hooting,
  Distant” by TheKingOfGeeks360: https://freesound.org/s/784609/
- `owl-hoot-anthousai-cc0.mp3` — “owl.wav” by Anthousai:
  https://freesound.org/s/398734/
