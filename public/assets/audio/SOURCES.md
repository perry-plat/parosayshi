# Audio sources

## Bird hover chirps

- Source: “Bird chirping sounds” by syncopika
- URL: https://opengameart.org/content/bird-chirping-sounds
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Local derivatives: `bird-chirp-blue.ogg`, `bird-chirp-brown.ogg`, and `bird-chirp-charcoal.ogg`
- Changes: Short calls were isolated from the three-second field recording, faded, filtered, normalized, and lightly pitch-varied for the three birds.
