# Audio sources

## Bird hover calls

The three hover sounds are short derivatives of natural field recordings by
[Sharadapte](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Sharadapte), published on
Wikimedia Commons under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The local derivatives are also offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.

- `indian-robin-call.mp3` — [Indian robin call and song recorded in February 2022 at Haripur, Sangli](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Indian_robin_call_and_song_recorded_in_February_2022_at_Haripur,_Sangli.wav)
- `house-sparrow-call.mp3` — [House sparrow call recorded in June 2013 at Sangli](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:House_sparrow_call_recorded_in_June_2013_at_Sangli.wav)
- `oriental-magpie-robin-call.mp3` — [Oriental magpie-robin call and song recorded in April 2023 at Haripur, Sangli](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oriental_magpie-robin_call_and_song_recorded_in_April_2023_at_Haripur,_Sangli.wav)

Changes: one foreground vocal gesture was isolated from each recording, cleaned
with light noise reduction and a gate to remove the surrounding soundscape,
given short edge fades, gently filtered, normalized, and encoded as MP3 for
reliable browser playback.
